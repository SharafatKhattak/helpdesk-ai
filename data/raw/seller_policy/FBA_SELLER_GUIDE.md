# Fulfilled by SoftStore — A Plain Guide for Shop Owners

If you sell on SoftStore.pk today, you pack every order yourself and hand it to the courier. Fulfilled by SoftStore (FBA) is a different way to sell: you send stock to us once, and we store it, pack it, ship it, and deal with returns for you. You stop touching those orders.

**Straight talk first: nobody has shipped an FBA parcel yet. Not one.** The screens exist and the fees are set, but enrolling a product is switched off for every seller until SoftStore opens its first fulfilment centre. Open the FBA screen on any of your products today and you'll find it — but pressing "enrol" tells you the programme isn't open yet. This guide explains what it is, what it will cost, and how it will work once it is — and is honest about what isn't ready.

## What FBA actually is

Think of it as giving your stock to a trusted manager in another city. You still own every unit — SoftStore never buys your stock from you, we just hold it (**amanat**, not a sale). We do the packing and the courier handover. You're still paid the same way: commission is only ever deducted when an order is delivered, same as normal selling. FBA fees are a separate, additional cost for the storing and shipping work.

You choose FBA product by product — nothing forces your whole shop in. You can run a mixed shop indefinitely: some items you pack yourself, some we pack, side by side.

## What it will cost

These are the real numbers already set in the system (not final pricing — the business hasn't fixed a permanent card yet — but they are what would be charged today if the programme were open):

**Sending stock in:** PKR 100 per shipment, plus PKR 15 for every unit we receive.

**Shipping each order**, charged by the weight of what you sell, not its value:

| Weight | Fee per order |
|---|---|
| Up to 500 g | PKR 60 |
| 501 g – 2 kg | PKR 90 |
| 2 kg – 5 kg | PKR 150 |
| Over 5 kg | PKR 280 |

**If a buyer returns something:** PKR 70 per return, plus PKR 10 per unit processed.

**If you want your stock back:** PKR 150 per request, plus PKR 25 per unit. Asking us to dispose of it instead costs less — PKR 50 plus PKR 15 per unit.

Your normal marketplace commission still applies on top of this when an order is delivered (see the commission table in `sellers/WHAT_YOU_CAN_SELL.md`) — FBA fees add to that, they don't replace it.

## How you will send stock in — once it opens

Enrolling a product will need a cost price, its weight, and its box dimensions, so the system can bill the right fee band — get these ready in advance if you're planning to use FBA. After that, sending stock in is a formal handover: you declare what's coming, and only stock we've actually counted and checked in at our end becomes sellable on the marketplace. Your word that something is "on the way" is never enough — it has to arrive and be checked first.

We can't tell you the exact steps, an address, or how long it takes, because no fulfilment centre exists yet to publish an address for. That isn't us withholding information — it genuinely doesn't exist. The moment it does, this guide is where it gets written down first.

## How you get stock back

You are never locked in. A product can be taken out of FBA at any time, back to selling from your own shelf. Once real stock is sitting in a centre, you'll be able to request it back on the same screen where you enrolled it — either returned to your shop or disposed of. A request is not an instant pickup: someone on our fulfilment team has to physically find, pack and hand your stock back before it counts as done.

## Who to contact

There's no separate FBA helpline yet — there's nothing running to ask about. For any question, including "when does this open," use **Help & Support** in your seller dashboard, the same place you'd raise anything else about your shop. We won't have a date for you yet, but we'll tell you honestly where things stand.

## Bottom line

FBA is a built feature waiting for its first fulfilment centre — not a running service. If anyone tells you your stock is already sitting in a SoftStore warehouse, that is wrong today. Until you are told otherwise here or in your dashboard, your own shop is the only place your stock exists. **آپ کا مال ابھی آپ کے پاس ہی محفوظ ہے** — your stock stays safe with you until this actually opens.
